Founder & CEO

Dr. Yuna Rashid

"Skin care is self care — and everyone deserves it."

After a decade working as a clinical dermatologist in Kuala Lumpur, Dr. Yuna noticed her patients struggling to find effective skincare that truly worked for tropical climates and diverse skin tones.

She founded BeYu in 2021 from her kitchen — literally — with a mission to create clean, affordable, and effective skincare inspired by Korean beauty philosophy but rooted in Malaysian identity.

Today, BeYu is Malaysia's fastest-growing skincare brand, loved by over 50,000 customers across Southeast Asia.

Answers

Frequently Asked

How long does shipping take?›
Standard shipping within Peninsular Malaysia takes 3–5 business days. East Malaysia is 5–8 business days. Free shipping on orders above RM 80. Express same-day delivery available in Klang Valley.
Can I return a product?›
Yes! We offer a 30-day return policy for unopened products. If your product arrived damaged or you received the wrong item, we'll replace it at no cost — even if opened.
Are BeYu products cruelty-free?›
Absolutely. BeYu is PETA-certified cruelty-free. We never test on animals at any stage of production. We're also working towards full vegan certification by 2026.
How do I find my skin type?›
Visit our Skin Type page and take our quick 2-minute quiz. We'll recommend a complete routine based on your answers. You can also book a free virtual consultation with one of our skin coaches.
What payment methods do you accept?›
We accept Visa, Mastercard, FPX (all Malaysian banks), Touch 'n Go eWallet, GrabPay, Boost, and Shopee Pay. All transactions are SSL-secured.
Can I use multiple products together?›
Yes! All BeYu products are formulated to work together. The recommended layering order is: Cleanser → Toner → Serum → Moisturiser → SPF (AM) or Night cream (PM).
